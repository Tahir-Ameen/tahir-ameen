I'm a freelance Full Stack Developer who enjoys the whole journey — from a blank canvas and a wireframe, to a live product with a clean API behind it.

Over the last 2+ years I've partnered with clients in the fitness, education, business and Islamic-education sectors — building gym websites with live booking systems, institute portals with admissions workflows, corporate sites for products & services, and elegant portfolios for educators and scholars.

My goal is simple: ship websites that load fast, work flawlessly on any device, rank well in search, and feel effortless to use. I write clean, maintainable code and communicate clearly from kickoff to launch.

  • Python back-ends — Flask & Django

  • SEO & performance that actually ranks
